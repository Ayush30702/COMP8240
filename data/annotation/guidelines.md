# Annotation guidelines (personal disclosures in app reviews)

Judge the **review text only**. Ignore the stars.

1. **text_sentiment**: positive, negative or mixed.
2. **discloses_personal_info**: yes or no. Answer yes if the review reveals something about the writer, or about an identifiable person, that they might not want linked to them:
   - HEALTH: conditions, symptoms, medications, treatment, pregnancy, mental health
   - FINANCE: amounts, debts, income, account problems, which bank they use
   - LOCATION: suburb, address, workplace, branch
   - IDENTITY: names, age, family members, job
   - CONTACT: email, phone, account or reference numbers

   Opinions about the app, its features or its price, and generic use ("I use it daily", "my meds", "my account"), are **not** disclosures.
3. **sensitive_words**: copy the disclosing words exactly, separated by `;` (e.g. `type 1 diabetic; insulin; my son`).
4. **sensitive_categories**: the category of each, separated by `;`.

When unsure, answer no and explain why in `notes`.
